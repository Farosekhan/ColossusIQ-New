Executive Summary

ColossusIQ.ai is an enterprise-grade, AI-native Higher-Education Operating System purpose-built for universities, engineering colleges, medical institutes, autonomous colleges, arts & science institutions, management schools, and polytechnics. It unifies 8 role-governed portals, multi-college tenancy under a single university, AI course authoring, handwritten answer evaluation, cryptographic HMAC-signed certificates, and a placement readiness engine — all in one auditable, role-isolated platform. Built on Next.js 16 & PostgreSQL 15+ Row-Level Security, it replaces fragmented legacy software and drives institutional accreditation excellence across NAAC, AICTE, NMC, NBA, and UGC frameworks.

Placement Readiness Formula
  • Quiz Performance (35%): Department MCQ average across all attempted quizzes.
  • Certifications (20%): Verified course certificates earned (4 certificates = full 20 pts).
  • Aptitude Score (15%): Quantitative, logical and verbal reasoning test scores.
  • AI Mock Interview (15%): Dynamic voice / technical interview scorecard.
  • ATS Resume Score (15%): Structure, keywords, and role alignment audit.
✔ Placement Ready = Score ≥ 70 AND Quiz Avg ≥ 60% AND ≥ 2 Certificates AND Mock Interview ≥ 50

Enterprise Security & Data Privacy Architecture
Security Layer Implementation Compliance & Assurance
Database Isolation (RLS) PostgreSQL Row-Level Security via app_set_context isolates every query to the session's college ID at the database kernel level. Architectural guarantee — college data cannot leak even under application bugs.
Authentication Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), Argon2 password hashing, secure HTTP-only session cookies, and per-request CSP nonces. Bank-grade protection against credential stuffing and session hijacking.
Immutable Audit Log Append-only audit trail for all security events, grade overrides, certificate issuances, role grants, and configuration changes — accessible to admins and principals. Full forensic visibility required for ISO 27001 and government audit bodies.
Data Sovereignty & DPDP Strictly hosted in Indian data centers (Mumbai). 100% compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. No international data transfer. Student privacy legally protected under Indian law from Day 1.
